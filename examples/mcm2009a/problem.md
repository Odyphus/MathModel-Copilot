# Traffic circle control historical example

This is an original educational interpretation of COMAP's 2009 MCM Problem A,
Designing a Traffic Circle. The official task asks for a control-selection model,
explicit objectives and influential factors, example applications, signal timing
when signals are recommended, and a short technical summary for an engineer.

Official source, read 2026-10-05:
https://www.contest.comap.com/undergraduate/contests/mcm/contests/2009/problems/

The official problem and student papers are not redistributed here. Obtain any
original contest materials from COMAP under their applicable terms. All demand
rates, capacities, routing assumptions and scenarios in this example are newly
authored synthetic data. They are not measured traffic or COMAP reference data.

This example evaluates a fluid approximation on a finite controller grid. It is
not a field-calibrated traffic model, road-safety assessment, universal optimum,
award prediction or formal contest submission.
