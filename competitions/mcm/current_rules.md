# MCM/ICM current rules baseline

> Last verified: **2026-10-05** against the published **2027** instructions. Re-check the official site at Stage 0 every contest year; COMAP's current instructions always take precedence.

## Official source

- [COMAP MCM/ICM Rules, Registration and Instructions](https://www.contest.comap.com/undergraduate/contests/mcm/instructions.php)

## Submission-critical checks for 2027

- The entire solution is limited to **25 pages**. The limit includes the Summary Sheet, solution, references, table of contents, notes, appendices, code, and problem-specific requirements.
- The paper must be in English, use readable type of at least **12 points**, and be submitted as one PDF.
- Page 1 is the Summary Sheet. Each solution page must show the team control number and page number in the header.
- Do not include student, advisor, school, or institution identity anywhere in the solution.
- The summary should be written last and should communicate the approach and most important conclusions rather than restating the problem.

## AI use

- Clearly disclose which AI tools were used and for what purpose.
- Cite AI-assisted content inline and list the AI tools in the reference section within the 25-page solution.
- Verify all AI-generated content and citations and correct inaccuracies, inconsistencies, or plagiarism risks.
- Append a section titled **Report on Use of AI** after the 25-page solution. That report is not counted toward the 25-page solution limit.

The empirical file deliberately contains no numeric distribution; it must never be used to replace the current official limit.
